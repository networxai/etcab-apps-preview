Fleet vs Partner: one app, two contents

Same frame, same tokens, same at-speed button. A fleet driver drives our car on a shift; a «Գործընկեր» drives their own car on their own time and keeps a prepaid balance («Հաշվեկշիռ»). Only partners have a balance. Only the content changes. Click a phone to open the live page.

All driver screens
IdenticalMap, status pill, offer, ride flow, morphing button, pre-orders, account, themes incl. Sun.
Differs: the carFleet picks a free car at shift open. Partner has exactly one approved car and no picker.
Differs: the moneyFleet sees only what it earned: no balance, no top-up, no debt. Partner sees a balance charged 15 % per ride by his tariff (Eco Comfort).
Differs: the blockBoth: expired insurance, until a new one is approved. Partner only: balance below 0. The current ride always finishes.
